# STRATEGY_THESIS

**Status: HYPOTHESIS ONLY.** Research focus documented 2026-10-11;
no calibrated entry, position rule, net expectancy or execution authorization.
Detailed definitions, gaps and backlog: [Movers and Levels](MOVER_LEVEL_STRATEGY_RESEARCH.md).
Backend evidence is from 55efaa2; it is not a code merge into the V2 branch.

## Objective
Study eligible Binance USD-M movers at levels, including a separately verified
small-cap cohort. +5%, +15% and +20%-70% moves are candidate research strata,
not entry thresholds or expected profits. Large 24h change is never an entry.
Ranking discovers candidates; liquidity/data quality gate eligibility;
level behavior and observable corroboration define the hypothesis.

## Hypothesis A - Continuation
Ignition/expansion may continue after a breakout/retest or controlled trend
pullback. Study known resistance becoming support and aggressive buying that
actually produces advance, with market context and liquidity recorded.
H-A1 breakout/retest is the first definition to preregister; H-A2 pullback is
secondary. No mandatory classifier state sequence or automatic EXPANSION entry.

Confirm only through positive net expectancy with uncertainty, acceptable risk
and chronological out-of-sample stability after executable cost/fill simulation.
Reject/defer if edge disappears after costs, depends on unavailable data,
concentrates in a few episodes/symbols, or cannot be reproduced outside sample.
A win rate below 50% alone does not reject a positive-expectancy strategy.

## Hypothesis B - Confirmed reversal
An extended rally can continue or exhaust. H-B1 studies failed highs, support
loss and a failed reclaim, with a level frozen at decision time and selling
that produces downside follow-through. Do not short because price rose 50%-70%.
EXHAUSTION UP is observed prior direction; it does not specify trade side.
Confirmation sacrifices the exact top; measure whether avoided false entries
compensate for delayed entry and adverse excursion.

Confirm/reject by the same net-expectancy, risk and external-validation criteria
as A. A negative forward mark return alone is not executable short profit.

## Secondary and deferred studies
H-C1 short continuation in losers is a separate secondary experiment.
H-D1 long rebound after extreme losses stays deferred until absorption and
structure can be tested; mirrors are not assumed statistically symmetric.
External catalysts are optional context with first-known timestamps, never
retrospective explanations inserted into decision-time features.

## From hypotheses to strategies
Current raw features, classifier, research setups and endpoint outcomes are
foundations. The next requirements are frozen levels/full snapshots/path
adequacy, preregistration, executable costed comparisons, then later paper.
See SIGNAL_RESEARCH_PLAN.md, EVENT_LABELING.md and FEATURE_BACKLOG.md.
Phase gates and authorizations remain in MASTER_PLAN.md and SUCCESS_GATES.md.
Documenting future work does not advance a phase or authorize any market run.

## Holding and risk
The minutes-to-about-one-hour human prior is a range to study, not a universal
exit timer or limit; current 4h endpoint observations do not prove tradable
long runs. Holding/exit rules consume post-entry evidence with a predeclared
maximum exposure duration and hard risk overrides. No averaging down,
leverage target or fixed numeric risk limit is introduced by this thesis.
